# Research Report

**Goal:** What is the current pricing for Anthropic's Claude API, including the newest models?

**Anthropic Claude API – pricing that is publicly listed as of the latest official sources (May 12 2026)**  

| Model (SKU) | Pricing tier | Input‑token price* | Output‑token price* | 5 M‑cache‑write price* | 1 h‑cache‑write price* | Cache‑hit/refresh price* |
|-------------|--------------|-------------------|---------------------|-----------------------|-----------------------|---------------------------|
| **Claude Opus 4.7** (and Opus 4.6 – same rates) | **Standard – Global (all regions)** | **US $5.00** per 1 M tokens | **US $25.00** per 1 M tokens | **US $6.25** per 1 M tokens | **US $10.00** per 1 M tokens | **US $0.50** per 1 M tokens |

\*All figures are **USD per 1 million tokens** (MTok) unless otherwise noted.  
The table above comes from the “Claude Model Pricing — All Platforms” PDF (effective 12 May 2026) which lists 14 model SKUs; the excerpt that is available to us contains the complete price breakdown for the Opus 4.7/4.6 SKUs.  

### Other Claude models (Sonnet, Haiku, Instant, etc.)

- The same PDF lists separate SKUs for Claude Sonnet, Claude Haiku, Claude Instant and earlier Opus versions, each with the same five‑column price structure (input, output, cache‑write, 1‑hour cache‑write, cache‑hit).  
- **Exact per‑token numbers for those models are not included in the excerpts you provided**, so we cannot quote them here. The official pricing page (`platform.claude.com/docs/en/about-claude/pricing`) states that those models are priced “per 1 M tokens” and that the table “shows pricing for all Claude models,” but the specific dollar values are missing from the supplied material.

### Regional pricing premium  

- Starting with the **Claude Sonnet 4.5, Haiku 4.5 and Opus 4.5** families (and any future models), Anthropic adds a **10 % premium for regional endpoints** (e.g., a Europe‑only or US‑only endpoint) over the default global endpoint price.  
- The global‑endpoint rates shown for Opus 4.7/4.6 therefore become **$5.50 / $27.50 / …** etc when a regional endpoint is used (10 % higher than the $5.00 / $25.00 base).

### How the pricing is billed  

- **First‑party Claude API** (the “Claude Platform” you call directly) uses the *global* pricing shown above.  
- **Marketplace‑operated clouds** (AWS Marketplace, Microsoft Foundry, Amazon Bedrock, Google Cloud) bill through **Claude Consumption Units (CCUs)**, but the conversion to USD is the same rates listed above.  

### Summary for the “newest” models  

| Model family (newest) | Base global price (per 1 M tokens) | Regional‑endpoint premium |
|-----------------------|-----------------------------------|----------------------------|
| **Claude Opus 4.7 / 4.6** | Input $5 / Output $25 (standard) | +10 % for regional endpoints |
| **Claude Sonnet 4.5**, **Haiku 4.5**, **Opus 4.5** (and later) | Same structure as Opus 4.7 (exact numbers not in the excerpt) | +10 % for regional endpoints |
| **Claude Instant** and earlier versions | Not listed in the excerpt | — |

> **What we can definitively state:** The only concrete API‑price numbers supplied in the gathered sources are those for Claude Opus 4.7 (and 4.6) as shown in the table above, with a 10 % regional premium for newer 4.5‑plus models. All other Claude model prices exist in the same PDF but were not part of the extracted text, so we cannot quote them without further source material.  

If you need the exact per‑token rates for Claude Sonnet, Haiku, Instant, or any other SKU, you will need to consult the full “Claude Model Pricing — All Platforms” document or the live pricing page (`claude.com/pricing`) where Anthropic publishes the complete table.
