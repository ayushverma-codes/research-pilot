## Day 1 – Old Delhi & the Historic Core  

| Time / Activity | Highlights (from “Top 15 Attractions” & “3‑Day Delhi Itinerary”) |
|----------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Morning**   | • **Red Fort** – start the day at this UNESCO World Heritage site.  <br>• Walk through **Chandni Chowk** on a historic food‑walk (the “Old Delhi food walk” mentioned in the itinerary guide). |
| **Mid‑day**   | • **Jama Masjid** (just a short walk from Chandni Chowk). <br>• Lunch at one of the iconic street‑food stalls highlighted in the “13 Most Famous Places In Delhi For Food” guide (e.g., famous chaats, kebabs, or parathas). |
| **Afternoon** | • **Raj Ghat** (Mahatma Gandhi’s memorial) or a quick visit to **Jantar Mantar** if time permits. |
| **Evening**   | • Explore the bustling **Khari Baoli** spice market (part of the heritage bazaar experience). <br>• Dinner at a heritage‑bazaars‑style eatery listed in the “18 Food Places in Delhi Through Heritage Bazaars and Trendy …” guide. |

*Transport tip:* The Red Fort and Chandni Chowk are both on the **Yellow Line** of the Delhi Metro (Chandni Chowk station). A day‑pass or reloadable smart‑card will let you hop on/off easily.

## Day 2 – New Delhi’s Monuments & Modern Highlights  

| Time / Activity | Highlights (from “Top 15 Attractions” & “3‑Day Delhi Itinerary”) |
|----------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Morning**   | • **India Gate** – start with a stroll around the war memorial and the surrounding Rajpath boulevard. <br>• **Rashtrapati Bhavan** (exterior view) and **Parliament House**. |
| **Mid‑day**   | • Visit **Qutub Minar** (the tallest brick minaret in the world) – featured in the “Top 15 Attractions” list. <br>• Lunch at a nearby café or street‑food stall mentioned in the “Best Food Places In Delhi: 7 Must‑Visit Restaurants, Cafes & Street …” guide. |
| **Afternoon** | • **Humayun’s Tomb** – a beautiful Mughal garden tomb (also part of the “14‑Day Delhi Itinerary” but easily fit into a half‑day). <br>• Optional: **Lotus Temple** (modern architectural icon) if you have extra time. |
| **Evening**   | • **Akshardham Temple** – a spectacular cultural complex that lights up after sunset (highlighted in the “Top 15 Attractions”). <br>• Dinner at a contemporary restaurant from the “Best Food Places In Delhi” guide (e.g., a modern Indian bistro). |

- **Qutub Minar** is reachable via the **Yellow Line** (Qutub Minar station).
