### Overall picture

* Anthropic’s official documentation groups its Claude models (Sonnet, Haiku, Opus) under a single pricing scheme, but the exact per‑token rates for most of those models are **not present in the gathered evidence**.  
* The only concrete, model‑specific rate we have is **$3 USD per 1 M input tokens for Claude 3.5 Sonnet**.  
* The “Claude Pricing 2026: Every Model, Every Tier, Full Breakdown” guide is referenced, but its detailed numbers were not extracted, so we cannot quote them here.  
* No pricing information for output tokens, batch discounts, prompt‑caching, or the higher‑tier subscription plans (Free, Pro, Max, Team, Enterprise) is included in the findings.

### Bottom line

Based on the sources you provided, the only verified price for a current Claude model is **$3 / 1 M input tokens for Claude 3.5 Sonnet**. The pricing for the other newest models (e.g., Claude Opus 5.5, Claude Sonnet 4.5, Claude Haiku 4.5) and for output tokens, tier‑specific rates, or volume discounts is **not available in the supplied material**. To obtain a complete, up‑to‑date price table you would need to consult Anthropic’s official pricing page or the latest “Claude Model Pricing — All Platforms” PDF directly from Anthropic.
