# Research Report

**Goal:** What is the current pricing for Anthropic's Claude API, including the newest models?

**Anthropic Claude API – Pricing snapshot (2026)**  

| Model (latest release) | Input‑token price* | Output‑token price* | Notes / current status |
|------------------------|-------------------|---------------------|------------------------|
| **Claude Fable 5**     | **$10 / 1 M tokens** | **$50 / 1 M tokens** | Highest‑capability, most expensive tier. |
| **Claude Opus 5** (or Opus 5.5) | **$5 / 1 M tokens** (Opus 5)  <br>**$4 / 1 M tokens** (Opus 5.5, announced Sep 2026) | **$25 / 1 M tokens** (Opus 5) <br>**$20 / 1 M tokens** (Opus 5.5) | Opus 5.5 is the newest Opus offering and is priced about 20 % lower on input and 20 % lower on output than Opus 5. |
| **Claude Sonnet 5**    | **$3 / 1 M tokens** (standard price)  <br>**$2 / 1 M tokens** (introductory price, ended Aug 31 2026) | **$15 / 1 M tokens** (standard) <br>**$10 / 1 M tokens** (introductory) | Introductory $2/$10 rate was scheduled to rise to $3/$15 on Sep 1 2026, but the increase **did not occur**; $2/$10 is now the permanent price. |
| **Claude Haiku 4.5**   | **$1 / 1 M tokens** | **$5 / 1 M tokens** | The most economical model, suitable for low‑cost, high‑volume tasks. |

\*Prices are quoted **per million tokens** (input + output). 1 M tokens ≈ 750 KB of text.

---

### How the pricing is delivered

| Delivery channel | Billing unit | How the cost is reported |
|------------------|--------------|--------------------------|
| **Anthropic‑operated cloud (Claude Platform)** | U.S. dollars per‑model token rates (as above). | Direct invoice or marketplace (e.g., AWS Marketplace). |
| **AWS Marketplace (Claude Platform on AWS)** | **Claude Consumption Units (CCU)** – each CCU = **$0.01**. | Anthropic first converts the per‑model token rates into USD, applies any negotiated discount, then translates the USD amount into CCUs for the AWS Marketplace bill. |
| **Partner clouds (Amazon Bedrock, Google Cloud, Microsoft Foundry)** | Pricing is mirrored from the Anthropic rates above, but the **cloud provider invoices you**.  Official pricing links for those clouds point back to the Anthropic rates. |

---

### Subscription tiers (plan names only)

Anthropic also offers **Free, Pro, Max, Team, and Enterprise** subscription tiers that bundle API access with additional features (e.g., higher rate limits, dedicated support, priority service).  The pricing tables above are the **per‑token rates that apply under any tier**; the tiers themselves do not change the per‑token price, but they affect quotas and service‑level guarantees.  No specific dollar amounts for the tiers were found in the supplied material.

---

### Discount & volume considerations

* **Batch API** – a flat **50 % discount** on the per‑token rates for asynchronous, high‑throughput workloads (mentioned in the “Claude Pricing 2026” overview).  
* **Negotiated discounts** – when the API is billed through the AWS Marketplace, Anthropic can apply custom discounts before converting the net USD amount into CCUs.  No published discount thresholds or tier‑based volume‑price tables were found in the provided sources.  

---

### Quick reference (per‑million‑token cost)

| Model | Input | Output |
|-------|-------|--------|
| Fable 5 | $10 | $50 |
| Opus 5.5 | $4 | $20 |
| Sonnet 5 | $2 (standard) | $10 (standard) |
| Haiku 4.5 | $1 | $5 |

*If you are using the Batch API, multiply the above numbers by **0.5** (i.e., 50 % off).*

---

### Bottom line

- **Claude Fable 5** – $10 / $50 per M tokens (input / output).  
- **Claude Opus 5.5** – $4 / $20 per M tokens (the newest Opus model).  
- **Claude Sonnet 5** – $2 / $10 per M tokens (current permanent rate).  
- **Claude Haiku 4.5** – $1 / $5 per M tokens (lowest‑cost tier).  

These are the latest, officially‑cited per‑token prices for Anthropic’s Claude API as of mid‑2026.  No additional fees, tier‑specific token pricing, or detailed volume‑discount tables were located in the provided findings.
