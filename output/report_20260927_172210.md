## Executive Summary

Anthropic’s latest public pricing (effective May 12 2026) sets the standard token rates at **$2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens for Claude Sonnet 5**. For the higher‑capacity **Claude Opus 5.5 (Fast mode)** the rates are **$8 per million input tokens and $40 per million output tokens**. No other model rates are disclosed in the available findings.

## Key Findings

- Claude Sonnet 5: $2 / 1 M input tokens, $10 / 1 M output tokens (standard price).  
- Claude Opus 5.5 Fast mode: $8 / 1 M input tokens, $40 / 1 M output tokens.  
- The $2/$10 pricing for Claude Sonnet 5, introduced as introductory pricing through August 31 2026, remains the current standard and will not increase on September 1 2026.  
- Anthropic operates a partner‑operated cloud platform where cloud provider invoices directly, and an Anthropic‑operated marketplace platform (e.g., AWS Marketplace) using Claude Consumption Units (CCUs).  
- The platform pricing documentation lists all models and effective dates but does not provide token rates for models other than Sonnet 5 and Opus 5.5 in the extracted findings.

## Comparison / Analysis

The data shows a clear tiered pricing structure: the base model Sonnet 5 offers the lowest token cost, while the higher‑performance Opus 5.5 commands four times the input rate and the same multiplier for output. This aligns with Anthropic’s typical model hierarchy where larger context windows and advanced capabilities are priced higher. The stability of Sonnet 5’s rate (no planned increase) suggests Anthropic’s pricing strategy focuses on long‑term predictability for developers, whereas Opus 5.5’s premium rates reflect its enhanced speed and capacity. Overall, the pricing reflects a standard cost‑performance gradient across the two models highlighted.
