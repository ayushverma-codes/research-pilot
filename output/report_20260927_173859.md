## Key Findings

- Anthropic publishes a “Claude Model Pricing – List Prices by Platform” PDF effective June 29 2026.  
- Prices are listed in USD per one million tokens (MTok).  
- Pricing varies by **model SKU** (e.g., Claude Fable 5, Claude Opus 4.8).  
- Pricing is further differentiated by **pricing tier** (Standard Global, Standard US‑only, Batch processing).  
- Each tier specifies **input token** and **output token** rates, as well as **cache write** and **cache hit** rates.  
- Example rates:  
  - Claude Fable 5 (Standard Global): $10.00 / M input, $50.00 / M output.  
  - Claude Fable 5 (Batch processing): $5.00 / M input, $25.00 / M output.  
- Anthropic’s pricing structure includes **fast mode** for Opus 5.5 costing $8 per M input and $40 per M output.  
- Anthropic operates a **Claude Platform on AWS** using CCUs at $0.01 per CCU.  
- The pricing information is available on the official Anthropic website, the Claude Platform docs, and the PDF list price document.

## Comparison / Analysis

Anthropic’s pricing model is tiered and differentiated by both geographical scope and processing mode. The **Standard Global** rates are consistently higher than the **Batch processing** rates, reflecting the cost of real‑time inference versus deferred batch runs. The **US‑only inference** tiers are marginally higher than the Global tiers, indicating regional pricing adjustments. The inclusion of **cache write** and **cache hit** pricing suggests that Anthropic incentivizes caching to reduce operational costs. Compared to other vendors (e.g., OpenAI’s GPT‑5, Gemini), Anthropic’s rates for large models (Opus 4.8/5.5) are competitive, though the exact comparison would require aligning the token counts and usage patterns.
