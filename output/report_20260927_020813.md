\* **Scope** – “Global” means the price applies to Anthropic‑operated (first‑party) endpoints that are available worldwide. “US‑only inference” is a higher‑priced tier for endpoints restricted to the United States; it carries a ~10 % surcharge.

### How the pricing works
* **All prices are quoted per 1 million tokens (MTok).**  
* **Input tokens** are the text you send to the model; **output tokens** are the text the model returns.  
* **Cache‑related charges** (5 M‑cache writes, 1 h‑cache writes, cache hits/refreshes) apply only when you use Claude’s optional caching features; they are also expressed per 1 M tokens.  
* **Batch‑processing** rates are roughly half of the standard rates and are intended for high‑throughput workloads that can send many requests together.

### Newer model families (Sonnet 4.5, Haiku 4.5, Opus 4.5)
Anthropic’s documentation notes that starting with **Claude Sonnet 4.5, Haiku 4.5 and Opus 4.5**, regional endpoints (US‑only) carry a **10 % premium** over the global endpoint rates shown above. The exact per‑token numbers for these 4.5‑series models follow the same structure as the 4.6/4.7 tables, but the official price list (PDF “Claude Model Pricing — All Platforms”) lists them separately. Because the PDF excerpt we have only contains the 4.6 and 4.7 rows, the precise token‑price values for the 4.5‑series are not reproduced here; the same pricing logic (standard vs. US‑only, standard vs. batch) applies.

### Where to verify / get the full list
* **PDF “Claude Model Pricing — All Platforms”** (effective May 12 2026) – contains the full 14‑model SKU table for every Claude model and every pricing tier.  
* **Platform documentation:** `https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/about-claude/pricing` – links to the same PDF and explains the regional premium and caching details.  
* **Claude‑by‑Anthropic website:** `https://claude.com/pricing` – provides an overview of subscription plans for the Claude UI (Free, Pro, Max, Team, Enterprise) but points developers to the API pricing table above for token‑based charges.

### Bottom line
- **Claude Opus 4.7** (the newest Claude model available via the API) costs **$5 / 1 M input tokens** and **$25 / 1 M output tokens** on the global endpoint, with a 10 % higher US‑only rate.  
- If you can batch requests, the cost drops to **$2.50 / 1 M input** and **$12.50 / 1 M output** (global).  
- All other newer families (Sonnet 4.5, Haiku 4.5, Opus 4.5) follow the same tiered structure, with a 10 % regional surcharge for US‑only endpoints.  

These are the most current, officially‑published prices for the Claude API. If you need the exact numbers for the 4.5‑series models or additional SKU‑specific details (e.g., region‑specific availability), consult the full PDF linked above or the platform pricing page.
