## Executive Summary

Anthropic’s current pricing for the Claude API, as of the latest official documentation, specifies that the newest Opus 5.5 model costs $8 per million input tokens and $40 per million output tokens. No other per‑token rates for newer models are disclosed in the available findings.

## Key Findings

- The Claude Opus 5.5 model is priced at **$8 per million input tokens** and **$40 per million output tokens**.  
- Anthropic offers multiple subscription tiers: **Free, Pro, Max, Team, and Enterprise**.  
- The platform bills through partner‑operated cloud providers (AWS Marketplace, Microsoft Foundry) using **Claude Consumption Units (CCUs)**, where **$0.01 equals one CCU**.  
- Official pricing documents are updated monthly (e.g., “Claude Model Pricing – List Prices” effective dates of 29 June 2026 and 12 May 2026).  
- Pricing is expressed in **USD per one million tokens (MTok)** unless noted otherwise.

## Comparison / Analysis

The available data centers only on the Opus 5.5 model’s token rates, indicating a clear tiered pricing strategy that distinguishes between input and output tokens. The CCU billing mechanism on AWS Marketplace suggests a unified billing approach across different cloud platforms, simplifying cost reporting for developers. However, the absence of explicit rates for other new models (e.g., Claude 4.6 or newer) means that the Opus 5.5 rate may represent the most current or publicly disclosed pricing, but it cannot be confirmed as the definitive price for all latest models.

## Limitations

- No per‑token pricing is provided for models other than Claude Opus 5.5.  
- The findings do not include detailed rates for the “Fast mode” or other feature‑specific pricing.  
- The documentation references CCU billing but does not translate those units into token‑level costs for all models.  
- There is no direct comparison of cost changes over time beyond the April 2026 note, so trend analysis is limited.
