## Executive Summary

Anthropic’s Claude API pricing in 2026 is tiered by model, with newer models such as Opus 5, Sonnet 5, Fable 5.1, and Haiku 4.5 charging higher per‑million‑token rates (e.g., Opus 5 at $8 M input / $40 M output) while legacy models remain cheaper (e.g., Haiku 4.5 at $1 M input). ## Key Findings

- Opus 5 (fast mode) costs **$8 per million input tokens** and **$40 per million output tokens** (Claude API blog, Sep 2026).  
- Haiku 4.5 starts at **$1 per million input tokens** (official per‑model rates, Sep 2026).  
- Sonnet 5 and Sonnet 4.6 are priced at **$3 per million input tokens** and **$15 per million output tokens** (official per‑model rates, Sep 2026).  
- Opus 5 and the Opus 4.x line are priced at **$5 per million input tokens** and **$25 per million output tokens** (official per‑model rates, Sep 2026).  
- Fable 5 (top‑tier) is priced at **$10 per million input tokens** and **$50 per million output tokens** (official per‑model rates, Sep 2026).  
- Claude Opus 4.7 (standard global tier) charges **$5 input / $25 output** per million tokens, with additional cache/write fees (e.g., $0.50 for 5 M cache writes).  
- US‑only inference adds a **10 % premium** to standard rates (e.g., Opus 4.7 US‑only: $5.50 input / $27.50 output).  
- Batch processing discounts halve the base rates (e.g., Opus 4.7 batch global: $2.50 input / $12.50 output).  
- Pricing tables list 14 model SKUs for the Claude API, each with tiered, regional, and feature‑specific pricing.

## Comparison / Analysis

The pricing structure reflects a clear hierarchy: newer, higher‑capacity models (Opus 5, Fable 5.1) command premium rates, while earlier, lighter models (Haiku 4.5, Sonnet 5) remain more affordable. Anthropic applies a 10 % surcharge for US‑only inference, indicating regional cost differentials. Batch processing offers substantial cost reductions—roughly 50 % off standard rates—encouraging large‑scale usage. Additional fees for caching and writes suggest that developers can further control expenses by managing token‑reuse patterns. Overall, the API pricing is granular, with multiple levers (model choice, region, batch discounts, caching) that affect total cost.

## Limitations

- No explicit pricing details were found for **Claude Instant**, **Claude 2**, or **Claude 3** models on the official site.  
- The findings do not include the **exact pricing for fast mode** on Opus 5 beyond the $8/$40 rates; other fast‑mode fees may exist.  
- Regional pricing beyond the 10 % US‑only premium (e.g., specific multi‑region or data‑residency pricing) is not detailed.  
- Prompt‑caching discount structures are mentioned but lack complete numeric breakdowns.  
- The PDF pricing table lists many SKUs, but the excerpt only shows Opus 4.7 and Opus 4.6; full pricing for all 14 SKUs is absent.  
- Any promotional or introductory rates that may be in effect are not captured.
